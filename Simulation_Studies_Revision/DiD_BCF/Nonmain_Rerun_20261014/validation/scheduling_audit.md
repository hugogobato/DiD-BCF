# Nonmain rerun scheduling and worker audit

The frozen nonmain manifest contains 9,227 fits, including eight completed bridge fits. After reuse, 9,197 simulation fits remain for Colab and 22 application fits remain for local execution. This reconciles to 3,999 diagnostic fits, 4,000 pretrend-impact fits, 1,198 sensitivity fits, and 22 applications. The original source manifest has 14,327 fits: the 5,100 main fits are already complete, and the eight bridge IDs are removed from the remaining work.

The main return is complete and passed its recorded validation: 4,844 returned fits plus 264 verified reused fits, for 5,108 total main and bridge fits across 64 archives. The earlier package scheduled 60 main notebooks in waves of 48 and 12, with two workers per notebook and a maximum predicted load of 7.974 hours. Its observed bridge archives completed all native and feature controls. The prior return supports using a 48-notebook maximum per wave for the new work; root owns the final bin assignments and load estimates.

For whole-fit elapsed times, the returned main N=200 effect q75 is 452.68 seconds at degree 1, 451.99 seconds at degree 2, and 451.73 seconds at degree 3. The returned degree-3 to degree-1 q75 ratio is 0.998, so the observed main effect fits show no degree-3 time increase. The bridge sensitivity fits at N=50 took 152.29 seconds at variance-prior rate 0.1 and 151.89 seconds at rate 10. The MPDTA unit-effect sensitivity fits took 880.12 and 878.63 seconds at those rates. The largest observed rate ratio is 1.003; a rounded 1.01 prior-rate multiplier covers the observed difference. These anchors support a narrow multiplier for rate alone, not every interaction with sample size or setting.

The current-budget diagnostic bridge took 564.26 seconds and peaked at 1,096 MB. The application pretrend bridge took 814.99 seconds and peaked at 1,127 MB. Both are single atomic BCFModel jobs using all three configured chains. Saved local pilot records at four-chain budgets took 1,407 to 2,306 seconds for the simulation diagnostic and 2,280 to 3,804 seconds for the application diagnostic, with peak RSS from 2.6 to 5.2 GB. Those pilot records are useful as cross-budget stress references; their warmup and retained-draw budgets exceed the current budget.

The frozen unit plan classifies simulation and application pretrend fits as atomic jobs. Each fit gets one unit with no chain index, and the worker calls the unmodified fit_pretrend once with num_chains=3. The fit passes the job variance prior into sigma2_global_shape and sigma2_global_scale, and collects model.tau_hat_train as the posterior draw matrix. The collector keeps the full draw matrix, documented as n_obs by num_chains times num_mcmc, and only publishes a fit completion after its summaries are written. The captured BCF defaults set minimum leaf size to five for both forests. The nonmain manifest pins the repaired engine build and patch hashes; its recorded numeric-tree prior globally restricts and normalizes the raw prior over minleaf- and maxdepth-feasible trees.

No current-budget degree-3 pretrend bridge exists. The proposed factor 1.4 for degree-3 pretrend fits is a conservative planning margin, rather than a measured runtime ratio. No sampled NPZ arrays were opened during this audit.

Grouped timing records are in scheduling_timing_summary.csv; input hashes, exact counts, measurements, and source-contract evidence are in scheduling_provenance.json.
