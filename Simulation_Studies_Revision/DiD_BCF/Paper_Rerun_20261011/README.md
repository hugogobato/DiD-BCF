# Repaired DiD-BCF main paper rerun

**Returned and complete, 2026-10-14:** all 60 main and four budget-check archives passed verification. All 5,100 main fits are collected. See the [results report](analysis_returned_20261014/RESULTS_REPORT.md), [comparison figure](analysis_returned_20261014/main_comparison.pdf) and [remaining-work plan](analysis_returned_20261014/NEXT_STEPS.md). Do not repeat completed main work. The separate `analysis_returned_20261014/collect_returned.py` adapter fixes automatic numeric conversion of four hexadecimal job IDs while preserving the sealed helpers and original result bytes. Returned ZIPs are currently in `Colab/`, with collected metrics in `Results/collected_20261014/`. The preparation instructions below describe the completed campaign.

Run the loose notebooks in [Colab](Colab/UPLOAD_GUIDE.md). There are 60 main notebooks: 48 in wave 1 and 12 in wave 2. Each targets approximately eight hours using two CPU workers. Four shorter notebooks cover eight pretrend, application and prior-sensitivity anchors. One additional notebook collects the returned metrics.

The main campaign replaces all 5,100 fits in the original main grid. It reuses 264 independently verified earlier repaired fits and assigns exactly 4,836 new fits. The panels, seeds, cohort specification and model priors are unchanged from the frozen job definitions. The repaired native source is pinned and checked, with IG(2,1) noise variance on standardized outcomes. The budget is GFR 50, no additional burn-in, 500 retained draws per chain, thinning 5 and three chains.

The budget decision follows the user's emphasis on paper metrics: the 264-dataset comparison found similar ATT, GATT and treated conditional-effect accuracy to historical raw results, with no consistent gain from B3. The earlier budget did not pass the previous full-state diagnostic gate. This campaign records a separate performance-focused policy and retains diagnostic evidence; it does not claim that the old gate passed. Coverage limitations will be reported alongside accuracy, and every assigned fit enters the evaluation.

Exports contain per-replication pooled posterior summaries and evaluation metrics, scalar diagnostics, and small JSON provenance records. They contain no posterior draw arrays, sweep traces, generated panels, native engines or copied source archives. CATT surface RMSE, MAE, MAPE, coverage and interval length are computed on the runtime before removing successful raw scratch. All application group, county-effect and contrast tables and all pretrend decision rows are exported by the new adapter. Thus final paper collection needs only the compact ZIPs.

Checkpoints are atomic complete fits. Enable `USE_DRIVE` to preserve them every 20 minutes. An interrupted fit restarts with its original seeds; completed fits are verified and skipped. The controller uses at most two workers with one numeric/OpenMP thread each, reduces concurrency under CPU or RAM limits, and reserves time to export before the 9.5-hour session deadline. If it cannot finish on a slower machine, resume the same notebook from its result ZIP.

Place returned ZIPs in [Results](Results/README.md). This directory and the 264 compact reused results are publishable final-campaign evidence and are not ignored. Earlier comparison archives, unpatched proper-prior Colab outputs and obsolete correction-completion results are retained locally under the repository's ignored `.local_experiment_archive/intermediate/` directory. Only that archive is newly ignored.

The eight smaller notebooks compare new earlier-budget summaries with the already-saved B3 anchors in `bridge_reference/`. These are budget checks, not additional Monte Carlo evidence for the main grid. After their results, decide budgets and generate the remaining full pretrend, application and sensitivity campaign. This delivery does not silently assign the earlier budget to those untested full phases. Updating the manuscript's numerical tables and figures follows completion and analysis of the final campaign.

The immutable job manifest, source hashes, scheduling inputs and assignments are in `manifest.json`, `package.json`, `preparation.json` and `assignments/`. Scheduling uses the 75th percentile of measured complete-fit elapsed times by sample size, a 20% margin and a setup reserve. Estimated hours assume two workers and are planning figures. The generator and bounded validation scripts are in `helpers/`; validation postprocesses existing draws and never launches production sampling.
