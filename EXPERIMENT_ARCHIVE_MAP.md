# Experiment archive map

The visible development archive is [`Old_Simulation_Studies/`](Old_Simulation_Studies/README.md). Its guide distinguishes original studies, null checks, superseded revision/pretrend notebooks, initial proper-prior reruns, earlier application results and development notes. Current model and experiment entry points are documented in the [repository README](README.md).

On 2026-10-27, superseded active-folder workflows were moved intact into that public archive. [REORGANIZATION_20261027.json](Old_Simulation_Studies/REORGANIZATION_20261027.json) records original/destination paths and per-file hashes. No experiment payload was deleted; the final campaigns, source seals, result overlays, benchmark inputs and publication assets stayed in place. Before-cleanup documentation is preserved under `Old_Simulation_Studies/Documentation_before_20261027/`.

The earlier [2026-10-14 migration record](Old_Simulation_Studies/Revision_DiD_BCF/MIGRATION_RECORD_20261014.json) documents relocation of the final main campaign and old correction/completion notebooks. Compatibility links preserve the former proper-prior/main campaign paths.

## Earlier local-only storage decision

Before this cleanup, some large pilot runtime/posterior-array ZIPs and intermediate packages had already been moved to `.local_experiment_archive/intermediate/20261011/` under the author's then-current instruction to keep them local. That pre-existing directory includes the 30-replication budget comparison, earlier unpatched proper-prior returns and correction-completion returns. It is distinct from the now-public `Old_Simulation_Studies` history and is not a source of current figure inputs. Its local `ARCHIVE_MAP.json` resolves original paths. No claim is made that all those local runtime artifacts are published.

The prior map is preserved at [Documentation_before_20261027/EXPERIMENT_ARCHIVE_MAP.md](Old_Simulation_Studies/Documentation_before_20261027/EXPERIMENT_ARCHIVE_MAP.md). All final paper metric collections and frozen source packages remain committed and available; none is moved into a gitignored archive by this cleanup.
